    Amber circles: predicted stars (planets, Sun and Moon discs, deep sky squares in a framed photo). Drag one onto its image on the photo to pin it
    Blue: pins. Drag to move, Shift moves at 1/4 speed; a disc pin's square handle sets its radius
    The fit runs after each pin change. All-sky: 6 pins at least (4 with span 0), 8 to 10 spread from the horizon to the zenith are best. Frame: 2 pins at least, 6 to 10 spread over the photo are best
    Drag the photo to pan, wheel or double-click to zoom; in a framed photo, Prediction (step 3) makes a drag shift the prediction instead
    Find stars: line the prediction up roughly (rotation, field, shift, or north), then let it pin the stars; it scans the rotation and, in a framed photo, the scale 15 % either way
    Del removes the selected pin, F fits the photo to the view
