# Light pollution grid (data/lightpollution/)

Night sky zenith brightness (mag/arcsec²) over the Earth, latitude -65° to 75°, generated by `tools/lp-grid.py` on 2026-11-06.

- **Radiance:** VNL_npp_2025_global_vcmslcfg_v2_c202604011200.average_masked.dat.tif (EOG VNL v2.2 2025 average_masked), Earth Observation Group, Payne Institute for
  Public Policy, Colorado School of Mines, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  International (CC BY 4.0). Elvidge, C.D., Zhizhin, M., Ghosh, T., Hsu, F.C., Taneja, J. (2021):
  Annual time series of global VIIRS nighttime lights derived from monthly averages: 2012 to 2019.
  Remote Sensing 13(5), 922.
- **Model:** the radiance averaged onto a 1/40° grid; each cell's artificial brightness the sum of
  the sources' radiance × area × K(d), K(d) = d^-n e^(-d/D), D = 100.0 km, cut at 250 km (the
  shape of the published approximations by Garstang, Cinzano and Falchi); scale and n = 1.95
  fitted to measured sky brightness (measured sky brightness, 115 sites in Europe with at least 3 measurements, rms 0.24 mag); the natural
  night sky (22.0) added as light.
- **Limits:** an estimate, not a measurement. VIIRS does not see blue light (white LEDs are
  undercounted) and measures the light going up, not the light thrown into the sky; the model has no
  terrain shading and one atmosphere everywhere; a few calibration points. Expect about ±0.5 mag.
- **Format:** byte v -> 16.0 + 0.05 v mag/arcsec² (255 no data); run-length pairs (length, value)
  by rows from the north, west to east, base64. Tiles of 10° at 1/40° (`lp-<lat>-<lon>.js`), a
  coarse grid at 0.1° for the globe (`lp-coarse.js`), the index (`lp-index.js`), a check picture of
  the coarse grid in the Bortle classes' colours (`lp-coarse.png`).
- **Sources** (`lp-src-<lat>-<lon>.js`, for the directional sky glow, js/lp-dir.js): the same radiance
  averaged onto a 1/20° grid, times the cell's area (km²), not spread; byte v -> 10^(top + (v - 254)·dex)
  with the strongest cell at 254 (`top`, `dex` in each tile and in the index), 0 no light, 255 no data;
  the same tiles of 10° and run-length coding.
